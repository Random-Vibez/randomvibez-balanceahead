Your data, your choice

Everything stays in this browser. Take a backup with you, or clear this plan when you are done.

Private by design. BalanceAhead saves only a bounded copy of this plan in your browser's local storage. It does not send your financial information anywhere. Exported files are unencrypted—keep them private. This is a planning aid, not financial advice.

A clear boundary

Private on this device

BalanceAhead has no accounts, backend, cookies, analytics, or network requests. Your plan stays in this browser's local storage. Anyone who can use this device may be able to see it.

Backups are plain JSON and are not encrypted. Store them somewhere private and delete them when they are no longer needed.

No login is needed because there is no server-side mutable user data or admin surface.
